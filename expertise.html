Expertise

Comprehensive Accessibility Evaluations

Comprehensive accessibility evaluations involve meticulous assessments of websites, mobile apps, and other digital platforms, ensuring compliance with WCAG 2.x standards while also striving to enhance the overall user experience for individuals with disabilities. Leveraging an experienced testing methodology that combines automated testing tools with manual assessments, barriers that hinder users with disabilities are effectively identified and addressed.

Each evaluation culminates in a detailed report outlining findings, severity levels, and prioritized recommendations for remediation. This approach emphasizes clarity and actionable insights, enabling teams to make informed decisions that significantly enhance accessibility across digital offerings.

Tailored Accessibility Consulting

Tailored accessibility consulting delivers personalized guidance and strategies designed to align with the unique needs and goals of each organization. By collaborating closely with teams, this process identifies specific accessibility challenges and opportunities, ensuring a thorough understanding of the digital landscape.

Through in-depth analysis and proactive stakeholder engagement, customized solutions are crafted to promote inclusive design practices and enhance user experiences. This collaborative approach empowers teams to seamlessly integrate accessibility principles into their workflows, cultivating a culture of inclusivity that drives lasting improvements in digital products and services.

Additionally, consulting services include practical training sessions that equip team members with the knowledge and tools necessary to maintain compliance and continuously improve accessibility. Ultimately, this tailored consulting approach paves the way for a more inclusive digital environment, benefiting both users and organizations alike.

Design-Driven Accessibility Reviews

Design-driven accessibility reviews emphasize collaboration between design and accessibility to create inclusive digital experiences. By working closely with design teams, accessibility evaluations of prototypes identify potential issues while offering effective workarounds that respect the designers' creative vision. This approach ensures that accessibility is integrated into the design process, enhancing usability without sacrificing aesthetic or functional quality.

The process culminates in a detailed Developer Implementation Guide. This guide is a crucial resource for development teams outlining essential aspects like names, roles, states, values, reading and focus orders, as well as instructions for managing keyboard focus in dynamic content. By providing both visual and accessibility requirements, this guide enables developers to create digital products that are visually appealing and accessible to all users.
